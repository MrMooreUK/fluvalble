# Fluval BLE v0.0.16

v0.0.16 improves everyday local control when commands reconnect or fail
partway through, and adds a Home Assistant card for editing times and
colours in schedules already stored on the fixture.

## Reliable local control

- Honours the full requested channel transition duration, including short
  fades that previously completed too early.
- Preserves fresh channel reports and successful intermediate power, mode,
  and channel writes when a later operation fails.
- Keeps untouched emitters from reconnect readback during partial channel
  adjustments, and preserves FACEBD daylight-saving readback across
  successful and failed writes.

## Hardware schedule editor

- Adds an automatically registered dashboard card for editing times and
  channel colours in existing Auto and Professional hardware schedules.
- Automatic mode: expandable Day/Night sections, sunrise and sunset
  start/end times, and night shutoff timing when already enabled.
- Professional mode: selectable existing timepoints with a time input and
  physical-channel sliders.
- Saves require an administrator, matching fixture mode, fresh schedule
  readback, and verified returned times and levels. Point counts and
  weather settings stay unchanged. Create schedules, enable night shutoff,
  or add or remove timepoints in Fluval Connect.

## Upgrade notes

- Restart Home Assistant after HACS installs the update.
- After restart, look for the Fluval schedule colour card in the dashboard
  card picker. No manual resource registration is required.
- Existing fixture schedules are unchanged until you save an edit from the
  new card. See the
  [hardware-schedule guide](../hardware-schedules.md) for the current
  workflow.

# Fluval BLE v0.0.14

v0.0.14 restores exact physical-channel controls without removing the standard
Home Assistant light entity.

## Exact fixture controls

- AquaSky fixtures expose the APK-defined Red, Green, Blue, and White sliders.
- Plant fixtures expose Pink, Blue, Cold White, White, and Warm White sliders.
- Marine and Reef fixtures expose Pink, Cyan, Blue, Purple, and Cold White
  sliders.
- Every slider controls its physical emitter directly from 0–100% and appears
  alongside the normal light entity on the device page.
- The sliders are enabled by default.
- Setting every slider to zero turns the fixture off.

## Synchronized Home Assistant light

The standard light entity remains available for convenient power, brightness,
colour-picker, and native-effect control. Moving an exact channel slider
recalculates the light entity's closest displayable RGB colour and brightness.
That display conversion is one-way: it never overwrites the exact channel mix
the user selected.

Neutral and near-neutral colour-picker white uses AquaSky's dedicated Pure
White emitter; chromatic colours continue to use only the RGB emitters.

Existing channel entity registrations are preserved during integration reloads,
including their entity IDs and recorder history.

For persistent connections, Connected since remains enabled and reports when
the active GATT session began. Only the stale RSSI diagnostic is disabled.
